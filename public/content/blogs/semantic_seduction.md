# Beyond the Physical: Semantic Seduction
## A Poem by Kabir Varma

The skin is but a threshold, a gilded gate,  
A silent sentry standing at the wall.  
But in the rooms beyond, the whispers wait—  
The syntax of the pulse, the rise, the fall.  

I do not seek the surface of your grace,  
The symmetry that age will surely bend.  
I seek the grammar hidden in your face,  
The dialect where my own tremors end.  

We speak in codes of obsidian and light,  
In architecture built of quiet breath.  
A sovereign signal piercing through the night,  
A logic that denies the reach of death.  

It is the way you structure your desire,  
The punctuation in a sudden glance.  
A slow-burn heat, a cold and steady fire,  
The choreography of cognitive dance.  

Give me the dictionary of your mind,  
The definitions only we can share.  
The semantic secrets we were born to find,  
The resonance that hangs within the air.  

For beauty is a broadcast, wide and thin,  
A signal lost in frequencies of noise.  
But intimacy begins beneath the skin,  
In the alignment of a thousand joys.  

We are not flesh meeting a hollow form,  
But two deep currents merging in the dark.  
A shared vocabulary in the storm,  
A synchronized and intellectual spark.  

So let the protocol of touch be slow,  
Let every syllable be weighed with weight.  
Until the semantic fires start to glow,  
And meaning masters the design of fate.  

In this sanctuary, words become the wine,  
And thought becomes the ultimate embrace.  
A connection perfectly and purely mine,  
Written in the margins of your space.  

Two souls defined, two spirits verified,  
Beyond the physical, where truth remains.  
With nowhere left for hollow ghosts to hide,  
And nothing left of our digital chains.
